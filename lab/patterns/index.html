How to read the results

The cutoff was 31 December 2025. Each row begins on the date its setup was complete. The changes are measured from that date’s Henry Hub cash price to the 1st, 5th or 10th subsequent observed quote. Missing days and holidays can lengthen the calendar interval.

Only episodes whose ten-quote outcome had ended before the cutoff qualify. The engine keeps the earliest qualifying date, then skips overlapping windows. For these rules, retained dates are at least 16 quotes apart. These historical prices use a frozen data edition and the comparisons are descriptive.
